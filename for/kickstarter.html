01What you get

Everything backers need after they pledge.

Access

Code-based backer access

Backers enter the code from your backers-only Kickstarter update to unlock the backer-only spaces. It checks the code, not the pledge itself, so the code should only ever appear in backer-only updates.

Support

A support desk

General support and commerce tickets kept out of public channels, with a support role that sees both queues.

Onboarding

A clear way in

Interest-based onboarding and a Server Guide, so a backer who joins mid-campaign knows where to go.

Feedback

Feedback with a status

Bug-report and suggestion forums with status tags, so backers can see what happened to what they reported.

Team

Staff & handoff

Moderation commands, escalation, appeals and lockdown procedures documented for your team.

After the campaign

A reason to stay

A reusable 14-day engagement plan — conversations, events and community contributions — for the weeks after the rush.

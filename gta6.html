The moment

The stream ends. The conversation can continue.

Not every viewer will join a Discord, and they don’t need to. The ones who want more should find a clear way in. Three situations come up again and again:

New viewers

They don’t know where to go.

Someone finds you mid-stream, enjoys the run and has nowhere obvious to go when you log off. A clear Discord link with a clear first step gives them a place to start.

Existing Discord

It’s confusing, or it’s quiet.

Twenty channels and three of them used. People join, look around and leave. Joey rebuilds around what your members actually use and keeps what already works.

Growing chat

It needs structure.

More viewers bring more questions, more spam and more moments that need a decision. Onboarding, moderation routes and clear owners keep it manageable for you and your mods.

Process

Six steps. You stay in control.

  1. Discovery callYour channel, your audience, your server today.
  2. Written scope & timelineWhat gets built, by when, and what is out of scope.
  3. Build in a test serverNothing changes on your live server yet.
  4. Your review & approvalYou walk through it and sign it off.
  5. ImplementationMoved onto your live server.
  6. Handoff or ongoing supportNotes for you and your mods, or a separately scoped monthly plan.

You keep ownership of your server. Joey works through a role you grant with only the permissions the work needs. He never asks for your account password or default Administrator access.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Do I need a large audience?

No. If you stream regularly and want a clear home for the people who already show up, it is worth doing. Audience size changes how much moderation the setup needs, not whether it is useful.

What if I already have a Discord?

Joey starts from it. He looks at what your members actually use, keeps what works and repairs the rest. You don't have to start over, and you keep the server.

Is this only useful for GTA VI?

No. GTA VI is a moment when a lot of viewers go looking for new creators. The community you build works for whatever you stream next.

Can you finish before launch?

That depends on the scope and on Joey's availability when you get in touch. The timeline is written into your scope before any work starts, so you know before you commit.

Do you supply live moderators?

Not by default. Joey sets up moderation roles, AutoMod and escalation routes, and can help you recruit and train moderators as part of a scoped project. Live moderation coverage is only available inside a separately scoped Community Partner agreement.

What does ongoing management cover?

Only what your written monthly scope lists: hours, coverage windows, number of events, moderation duties and a monthly report with member and message counts. Anything else is agreed separately.

Will I own the server?

Yes. You keep ownership. Joey works through a role you grant with only the permissions the work needs, and never asks for your account password or default Administrator access.

Can you guarantee growth?

No, and nobody honestly can. Joey builds the structure that gives viewers a way and a reason to take part between streams. How many of them join depends on your content and your audience.

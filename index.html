06Operating doctrine

Analyze. Structure. Activate. Sustain.

01 / Analyze→

Find the real friction

Map member behaviour, dead ends, staff load, onboarding gaps and the reasons people stop showing up. Fix the actual system, not the symptoms.

02 / Structure→

Build the machinery

Channels, roles, governance, progression, security, staff ownership and operating docs — designed as one connected system.

03 / Activate→

Create a reason to return

Launch momentum, first interactions, events, feedback loops and outreach that turn a server from technically open into socially alive.

04 / Sustain↻

Make it survive you

Train the team, distribute ownership, measure retention and keep improving until the community can run without one person holding it together.

07EngagementsThree problems I own end-to-end
01 / Launch→

Launch a community

Turn an empty server into a place with a structure, identity and opening rhythm people can actually enter. Server architecture & roles, launch plan & opening events, onboarding & first-contact flow, rules, moderation & security, initial staff structure.

02 / Repair→

Fix a dying community

Find where people drop out, rebuild around real behaviour and create reasons for members to return consistently. Community audit & diagnosis, retention & channel restructure, feedback and reactivation loops, events & engagement systems, growth & partnership plan.

03 / Scale→

Build a team that runs it

Replace founder-dependence with clear ownership, trained staff and documented systems that survive handoff. Staff roles & departments, recruitment & application flows, training & operating standards, escalation & accountability, clean handoff & continuity.

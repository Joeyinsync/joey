INTEGRATION
PRIVACY

TikTok integration · Privacy Policy · Effective 20 October 2026

Contents

This policy explains how the private TikTok integration operated by Nikolaj Krištof Jochym (“Joey”), a private individual, handles data. Joey is the controller. Contact: joey@joeyinsync.com. The policy for the joeyinsync.com website itself is the separate website privacy policy.

Section 01What this covers

The integration is a TikTok developer application used only by Joey to read data from his own TikTok account. It is not available to anyone else, and it does not collect data about other TikTok users beyond what TikTok includes in the authorised account's own data.

Section 02Data accessed

With the scopes user.info.basic, user.info.stats and video.list, the integration can read:

  • Basic profile information: display name, avatar and the account's TikTok identifiers.
  • Account statistics: follower, following, like and video counts.
  • The account's public video list: video titles and descriptions, cover images, links, publish dates, and view, like, comment and share counts.

It does not access private messages, drafts, payment information, contacts or the content of other users' accounts, and it cannot post or change anything.

Section 03How it is used

The data is used only for Joey's personal analysis of his own TikTok account: reviewing statistics, comparing video performance and planning content. It is not sold, shared for advertising, or used to make automated decisions about anyone.

Section 04Services involved

  • TikTok provides the data through its developer API under the TikTok Privacy Policy.
  • Composio manages the connection: it stores the OAuth access tokens and makes the API requests on Joey's behalf. See the Composio privacy policy.
  • Claude by Anthropic, the AI assistant Joey uses, receives the data that Joey asks it to analyse. See the Anthropic privacy policy.

No other parties receive data from the integration. These providers may process information outside the European Economic Area under the transfer safeguards described in their own policies.

Section 05Storage & retention

Data is fetched on request when Joey wants to review his account; the integration does not run its own database. Composio keeps the OAuth tokens for as long as the account stays connected. Copies of results that appear in Joey's AI conversations are kept according to his settings with that service and can be deleted there.

Section 06Revoking & deleting

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions, or by disconnecting the account in Composio, which removes the stored tokens. After that, the integration cannot read any further data. Requests to delete data can be sent to joey@joeyinsync.com.

Section 07Rights & contact

Under the GDPR you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data, object to processing, and complain to the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic. This policy may be updated if the integration changes; the date at the top shows the latest version. Contact: joey@joeyinsync.com.

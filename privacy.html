This notice covers the portfolio at joeyinsync.com. The controller of personal data processed through this site is Nikolaj Krištof Jochym, acting as a private individual and not as a business — see the imprint. Email is the only contact channel. For a privacy request, write to joey@joeyinsync.com.

Section 01Visiting this portfolio

The portfolio has no account registration, contact forms, checkout or advertising trackers. Visitor analytics run only if you accept them (see section 02). Your choice itself is saved in your browser's local storage so the banner does not ask again; it is not sent anywhere.

GitHub Pages hosts the website. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security. Requests to deliver pages and assets also involve technical information such as browser/request details. See GitHub's privacy statement for its processing and retention practices.

Typefaces, stylesheets, scripts and animation libraries are all served from this website itself rather than from Google Fonts or any third-party CDN. The only request to another company is Google Analytics, and only after you accept it.

Section 02Analytics & cookies

If you click Accept in the cookie banner, the site loads Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC. It measures which pages are visited and how: pages viewed, time on page, referring site, approximate location (country and city), device, browser and operating system. Google Analytics 4 does not store visitors' IP addresses. Advertising features and ad personalisation are switched off, and the data is used only to understand how the portfolio is used.

Google Analytics sets the cookies _ga and _ga_3QCQLSF911, which distinguish visits and last up to two years unless you delete them. The legal basis is your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and the consent required for cookies under Slovak electronic communications law. If you click Decline or make no choice, Google Analytics is not loaded and no analytics cookie is set.

You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings in the footer of every page; declining removes the analytics cookies. Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in Google Analytics and then deleted by Google. Google may process it outside the European Economic Area under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; see Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Section 03When you get in touch

If you email Joey, the information you choose to send—such as your email address, name, message and attachments—is processed to read and respond to your enquiry. Mail sent to joey@joeyinsync.com is forwarded by Namecheap, the domain registrar, to a Gmail inbox, and Gmail provides the email service. Please share only information relevant to your enquiry, and avoid sending passwords or sensitive personal information.

Responding to general enquiries relies on the legitimate interest in communicating with people who contact Joey. Where an enquiry concerns entering a contract with you, necessary processing may instead be based on taking steps at your request before a contract. Information needed to comply with a legal obligation is processed on that basis.

Enquiry information is needed for handling the enquiry and any resulting relationship. Any further retention depends on outstanding follow-up, applicable legal recordkeeping duties or the need to establish or defend legal claims. You can ask about the information held about your enquiry and request deletion where applicable.

Section 04Portfolio references

Client-provided testimonials, display names and profile pictures are published on the basis of consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. A record of each consent is kept so that it can be demonstrated under Article 7(1). Consent can be withdrawn at any time, with no effect on processing carried out before withdrawal: write to the contact email above and the testimonial is removed.

Section 05External services

LinkedIn, Fiverr, Upwork, Calendly and other external destinations are links, not embedded widgets. Following a link takes you to a separate service whose privacy terms apply. Messages sent through those services are handled within their respective platforms. This notice does not cover another firm's invoicing or contracting activities.

Hosting and communication providers may process information outside the European Economic Area. Their privacy notices explain the locations and safeguards they use, including applicable transfer mechanisms. Contact Joey for questions about providers involved in your enquiry.

Section 06Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing; for analytics, use Cookie settings in the footer. You may also complain to the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic or your local data protection authority.

Contact joey@joeyinsync.com to make a request. No automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects are made by this portfolio.
